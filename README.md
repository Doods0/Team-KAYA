# Concept file Team KAYA's game

## Visual Identity

- **Theme :** Gothic, spiritual but vibrant and somewhat wholesome.
- **Map :** A magical forest full of bushes and mushrooms, with a blue-ish eerie tint.
- **Enemies :** Enemies are either dead, possessed, magicians or just corrupted entities, they all keep a somewhat wholesome look.
- **Name suggestions :** Deadclock, Timeworn, Thicketock, Time Reaper, Endless Dawn.
- **UI :** Minimal HUD, easy-to-read shop menu.

## Gameplay

### Core Loop

- Endless waves that escalate according to how long you survive.
- The player manipulates time, either making it move faster or slower.
- Time slows down passively.
- Killing enemies speeds time up.
- Time too slow → lose condition.
- Time too fast → enemies become faster accordingly, increasing damage risk → lose condition (death).
- Two losing conditions : Death from damage and from slow time.
- Passive time slowness rate is increased the longer you survive, time manipulation pickups are buffed as well, making the balance more challenging the more you survive.
- Players are meant to follow a strategy of combining different upgrades and different weapons and buffs to control the number of enemies attacking and maintain time stability as long as possible.

### Weapons

**Heavy Melee**
- Higher damage, lower range.
- Kills happen in close range, making pickups almost inavoidable.

**Light Weapon**
- Dual mode, switchable via keybind:
  - Throw (ranged)
  - Slash (melee-range)
- Lower damage than heavy melee.
- Kills can happen at far range, making pickups avoidable.
- Slash-mode range: between heavy melee and throw range, can hold tempting buffs.

### Pickups

- Pickup auto-collected only if kill occurs within player's pickup radius.
- Dropped by enemies on death.
- Types:
  - Speed-up (time)
  - Slow-down (time)
  - Shop-access pickup (rare drop)
  - Points (Used in shop)
- Enemies are color-coded based on the pickup they drop.
- Shop-access pickup : triggers shop UI instead of death when time gets too slow, lets you proceed after shopping.

### Shop & Upgrades

- The player can find and purchase weapons, either light or heavy.
- Different weapons vary in damage and base cooldown and may have special abilities (shooting a beam on 4th hit).
- There are specific upgrades for the heavy and light weapons, but they all apply no matter the weapon.
- Weapons can be changed, upgrades remain.
- There are general upgrades that affect the player's defense and overall progression.
- Some upgrades may be more beneficial together (Light weapon may give curse, heavy weapon may give double the damage for cursed enemies).
